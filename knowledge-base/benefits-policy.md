## 2. Health Insurance

Eligible full-time employees are provided with company-sponsored health insurance.

The standard health insurance plan covers the employee.

Coverage for eligible dependents may be available according to the selected insurance plan.

Employees should contact People Operations for details about dependent coverage.

## 3. Life Insurance

Eligible full-time employees receive company-provided life insurance coverage.

The coverage amount is **₹10,00,000**.

Additional voluntary coverage may be available depending on the insurance provider and applicable company policy.

---

## 4. Retirement Benefits

Eligible employees may participate in the company's retirement savings program.

The company provides a matching contribution of **5% of the employee's eligible base salary** when the employee contributes to the program according to the applicable plan rules.

Employees should contact People Operations for eligibility and enrollment details.

---

## 5. Learning and Development

Employees may receive an annual learning and development allowance of **₹25,000**.

The allowance may be used for approved professional development activities such as:

* Technical courses
* Professional certifications
* Conferences
* Workshops
* Job-related learning materials

Expenses must meet the company's reimbursement requirements.

## 6. Internet Allowance

Employees approved for regular remote work may receive an internet allowance of **₹1,000 per month**.

Eligibility is subject to the company's remote-work policy.

Employees should submit any required documentation through the designated expense process.
