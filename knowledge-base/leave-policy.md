# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the fictional company's employee leave policy.

This document is part of a personal QA portfolio project created to demonstrate AI/LLM testing, RAG evaluation, response validation, and hallucination testing.

**Note:** NovaTech Solutions is a fictional company created for testing purposes.

---

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to **20 paid annual leave days per calendar year**.

Annual leave should normally be requested in advance through the company's designated leave management system.

Approval is subject to business requirements and manager approval.

---

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness may request sick leave.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when they are unable to attend work because of illness.

Supporting medical documentation may be requested according to company policy.

---

## 4. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may apply for parental leave in accordance with applicable company policy and local legal requirements.

The exact duration and eligibility requirements may depend on the employee's circumstances and applicable regulations.

Employees should contact People Operations for detailed eligibility information.

---

## 5. Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave when they have exhausted applicable paid leave or when unpaid leave is appropriate for their circumstances.

Unpaid leave requires manager approval and may also require People Operations approval.

---

## 6. Leave Carry-Forward

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following calendar year subject to company policy.

The maximum number of days that may be carried forward is **5 days**.

Any leave carried forward must be used within the period specified by company policy.

---

## 7. Leave Request Process

Employees requesting leave should:

1. Submit a leave request through the designated leave management system.
2. Select the appropriate leave type.
3. Enter the requested dates.
4. Provide any required information or supporting documentation.
5. Obtain the required approval before taking planned leave.

---

## 8. Emergency Leave

In an emergency situation, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

The employee may submit the formal leave request after notifying their manager.

---

## 9. Leave Cancellation

An approved leave request may be cancelled or modified subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Employees should update the leave management system when a planned leave is cancelled.
