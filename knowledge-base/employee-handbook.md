# Employee Handbook

## 1. About the Company

This document contains general employee information for the fictional company **NovaTech Solutions**.

This document is part of a personal QA portfolio project created to demonstrate AI/LLM testing, RAG evaluation, response validation, and hallucination testing.

**Note:** NovaTech Solutions is a fictional company created for testing purposes.

---

## 2. Working Hours

The standard working schedule is:

* Monday to Friday
* 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
* One hour lunch break

Employees working in different business functions may have different schedules based on their team requirements.

---

## 3. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely when their role and team requirements allow it.

Remote work eligibility is subject to company policy and manager approval.

Employees should remain available during their assigned working hours while working remotely.

---

## 4. Employee Identification

Employees are provided with a company identification card.

Employees should carry their identification card while working from a company office.

Lost identification cards should be reported to the People Operations team.

---

## 5. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to:

* Treat colleagues respectfully.
* Maintain professional communication.
* Protect company and customer information.
* Follow applicable company policies.
* Report suspected security or compliance issues through the appropriate internal channels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and inappropriate workplace behavior are not permitted.

---

## 6. Information Security

Employees must protect confidential company information.

Employees should:

* Use approved company systems for business activities.
* Protect passwords and authentication credentials.
* Avoid sharing confidential information with unauthorized people.
* Report suspected security incidents promptly.

Employees must not intentionally disclose confidential company information to unauthorized parties.

---

## 7. Performance Reviews

Employee performance reviews are conducted periodically.

Performance evaluations may consider factors such as:

* Quality of work
* Delivery of responsibilities
* Collaboration
* Communication
* Professional development

The exact timing and process of performance reviews may vary by company policy.

---

## 8. Employee Support

Employees can contact the People Operations team for questions related to:

* Leave
* Benefits
* Employee records
* Workplace policies
* General employee support
